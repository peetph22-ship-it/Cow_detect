1 Executive Overview & Design Intent

The public-facing pages/index.vue is the central digital gateway of CowCare AI. Rather than functioning merely as marketing copy, it is engineered as an Enterprise-Grade Agritech Showcase and Interactive Scientific Sandbox.

4 Security & Access Control Architecture

Zero Administrative Exposure on Public Surfaces:
In accordance with security standards, all direct references and navigation links to /Admin ("Command Center") have been stripped from the public landing page. All visitors authenticate through /login.
  • Layout Guard (frontend/app/layouts/admin.vue): Automatic verification of authentication token and role claims. Users without the role === 'admin' property are redirected immediately to /login.
  • User Interface Preparation: Establishes the foundation for the upcoming general member dashboard (/user), maintaining strict role segregation.
